✅ WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED

Your hyperopt system now optimizes 3 ADAPTIVE risk management features:

1. 🎯 ADAPTIVE SIGNAL THRESHOLD
   • Adjusts based on market volatility (ATR)
   • High volatility → Higher threshold → Trade less → Avoid chaos
   • Low volatility → Lower threshold → Trade more → Capture opportunities
   
2. 🎯 ADAPTIVE STOP LOSS  
   • Adjusts based on ATR (Average True Range)
   • High ATR → Wider stop loss → Less whipsaw
   • Low ATR → Tighter stop loss → Better risk control
   
3. 🎯 ADAPTIVE TAKE PROFIT
   • Adjusts based on volatility
   • High volatility → Higher targets → Capture big moves
   • Low volatility → Normal targets → Lock profits sooner
